# Plan — ULW-Research: Prior Work on LLM Edit Tools/Formats

Tier: HEAVY (user demanded deep/exhaustive research). Deliverable: cited Korean markdown survey report + chat synthesis, mapped onto pss-edit-format-bench fairness. Evidence: session journal (.omo/ulw-research/<ts>/), debate log, verification outputs.

## Ordered steps
1. Bootstrap: scope searches (done), brief + journal scaffold (done), plan file (this), goal, notepad, todos.
2. Wave 1: team_create (5 members) + 4 librarian lanes in one turn. VERIFY: all spawned, run_in_background, unique angles.
3. Journal each return as it lands: digest + EXPAND markers → wave-<N>-<axis>.md; seed observation-manifest + claim-graph from CLAIMS markers.
4. Expansion: route every new lead to owning member or new lane; expansion-log dedupe; ≥2 waves; converge when zero unchecked leads OR 3 dry waves.
5. Debate: skeptic ATTACK rounds on every contested/high-risk claim; relay defense; verdict → debate-log → claim-graph.
6. Verify contested code-shaped claims by execution (verify-<slug>.md).
7. Lock non-code claims: 2+ independent domains, 2+ observation groups, counter-search, primary source, temporal evidence → verified-claims digest.
8. Synthesize: re-read journal; write SYNTHESIS.md (exec summary, findings by theme, ranked sources, verified claims, debate record, contradictions, gaps, expansion trace).
9. Korean report: repo artifact mapping findings onto pss-edit-format-bench fairness (write to experimental/pss-edit-format-bench/RESEARCH-EDIT-TOOLS.md).
10. Teardown: team_delete(force) + confirm every lane terminal; final answer with checklist.

## Stop rules
- STOP when: every axis covered by a member/lane; every EXPAND lead investigated or closed; every contested claim debated (or moved to unresolved/refuted annex); every synthesis claim cited; claim graph complete with convergence status; SYNTHESIS.md + Korean report written; team disbanded; final answer delivered.
- Pause-and-ask at expansion depth 5 waves with open leads.
- After 2 identical failed attempts at one step → surface + ask.
